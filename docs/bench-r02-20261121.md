[English](bench-r02-20261121.md) | [Русский](ru/bench-r02-20261121.md) | [Documentation](README.md)

**R0.2 accepted.** Three consecutive S1–S5 runs differ by at most 4.75 % on
every declared mean; the limit is 5 %. All 15 scored scene runs completed.
The source snapshot and the executable SHA-256 matched before and after the
series. This series is the R0.2 evidence. [R0.6](product-roadmap.md#rebuild)

## Observations

- S1 and S2 also met their absolute budgets (mean and p95 ≤ 16.67 ms; S1 peak
  working set about 250 MiB). Their ~119 FPS is the Player's own frame cadence;
  the Player render costs about 2.0 ms (S1) and 1.2 ms (S2).
- S3–S5 are dominated by the legacy CPU vertex expansion: every frame writes
  28.5 MB (S3), 56.8 MB (S4) and 4.32 GB (S5) of expanded vertices in one draw
  call. R1/R2 target exactly this cost.
- S4 drew 105 primitives and 262,961 triangles per frame (Sponza: 103 and
  262,267). The 256 point lights are inventory only until R5. The three
  alpha-mask materials are opaque and base-color textures are at most 256 px,
  as described in [bench.md](bench.md). S4 p95 was 43.6, 42.1 and 55.6 ms.
- The S3 ECS tick spread (4.75 %) is the closest to the limit because its
  absolute value is under 1 ms. Keep the machine idle for R0.6.
- S5 completed 30 measured frames of 20 million triangles per run; its peak
  working set was 12.8 GiB.
- GPU pass times, the S4 reference-image difference and meshlet counts stay
  unavailable until R0.3, R5 and R8.
